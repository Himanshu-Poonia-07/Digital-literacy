Responsible Social Media Use: A Checklist for College Students

**Dos**

* Think before you post. Make sure what you share is okay and respectful.

* Keep your info safe. Use passwords and change your privacy settings.

* Check if something is true before you share it. Don't spread news or rumors.

* Share things that show who you are and what you care about. Keep your presence positive.

* Be kind to others online. Don't make mean comments.

**Don'ts**

* Don't share stuff like your address or phone number online.

* Never be mean, to others online. Don't. Support hurtful content.

* Don't share much about your life. Keep some things to yourself.

* Don't post when you're angry or upset. You might regret it later.

* Don't believe everything you see online. Watch out for scams, fake news and accounts that aren't real.
