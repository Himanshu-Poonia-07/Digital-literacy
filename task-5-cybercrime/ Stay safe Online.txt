Stay Safe Online, Prevention Checklist  
Always verify links before clicking, especially those received through email, SMS, or WhatsApp.  
Do not share your passwords, OTPs, or personal details with anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank or official service.  
Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on all important accounts like email, banking, and social media.  
Use strong and unique passwords for different accounts. Avoid using the same password for all your accounts.  
Be cautious when using public Wi-Fi in cafes or on college campuses. Avoid logging into banking apps on unsecured networks.  
Keep your phone and apps updated to protect against security risks.  
(UPI Safety) Always double-check the UPI ID or QR code before making any payments.  
(UPI Safety) Never approve “collect requests” (Request Money) from unknown contacts on apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm.  
Avoid downloading apps or files from unknown or unofficial sources.  
Log out from shared devices after use, especially in college labs or libraries.
